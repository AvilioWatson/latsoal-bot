## Anti-pattern verdict

The interface passes the AI-slop test. The cream editorial palette, orbital motif, typography, and half-screen review panel feel intentional and project-specific. The deterministic scan found two issues: numbered section scaffolding in `frontend/import.html` and a layout-property `width` transition in `frontend/styles.css`. The dashboard's six equal metric blocks also approach the generic hero-metric pattern, but the overall product remains distinctive.

## What's working

- The homepage establishes a memorable identity without obscuring its single action.
- The Bank Review split pane keeps the source list and review context visible together.
- Page shells, typography, navigation, empty states, and reduced-motion handling are consistent.
- Import provides unusually strong scaffolding through examples, prompt generation, validation, and staged rendering.

## Priority issues

- **P1: AI fallback is visually and operationally treated as AI approval.** A provider failure can produce `fallback_used: true` and `lolos: true`; the UI says it passed AI review and enables Approve. Distinguish provider-reviewed, locally validated, and manually approved states. A fallback must never satisfy the AI-review gate.
- **P1: Text contrast misses WCAG AA.** `--faint` is about 2.4-2.7:1 on common surfaces, and gold text/button combinations are around 3.4:1 while used at small sizes. Replace faint text tokens for meaningful copy and create accessible foreground/accent pairs.
- **P1: Mobile touch targets are consistently undersized.** Many navigation links, filters, mini buttons, and preview actions measure 24-36 px high. Raise interactive targets to at least 44 px or provide equivalent hit-area padding.
- **P2: Primary workflows lack progressive disclosure.** On mobile Generator, taxonomy add/delete panels appear before Level, Mode, Provider, Account, and Generate. Move taxonomy administration into a separate settings surface or collapsed advanced section. In Bank Review, group secondary actions into an overflow menu and keep one clear primary review action.
- **P2: Card semantics contain nested interactive controls.** Each saved card is a `role="button"` while containing another button, selects, and a save button. Use an ordinary article with a dedicated preview button/link so screen-reader and keyboard behavior stays predictable.

## Persona red flags

- **Alex, power user:** no shortcuts for Generate, Save, Approve, next/previous item, or preview close; repeated one-card classification controls create visual and tab-order noise.
- **Sam, accessibility-dependent:** low-contrast metadata, undersized controls, an unnamed status select, and nested button semantics complicate keyboard and screen-reader use.
- **Casey, mobile user:** wrapped top navigation consumes substantial vertical space, taxonomy administration delays the primary Generate action, and several controls are too small for reliable one-handed use.

## Cognitive load

Generator and Bank Review fail five of eight checks: single focus, chunking, one-decision-at-a-time, minimal choices, and progressive disclosure. Grouping and context retention are strong, especially in the split preview, but the interfaces expose expert controls before they are needed.

## Recommended direction

1. Harden review-state semantics and approval gating.
2. Fix contrast and touch targets as a shared token/component pass.
3. Distill Generator taxonomy administration and Bank Review action hierarchy.
4. Add accessible focus management and simpler card semantics.
5. Finish with a responsive and reduced-motion polish pass.
